VoiceLink AI Translator

★5.03 ratings

What you say
never leaves the device.

VoiceLink is a live interpreter that runs 100% on your iPhone or iPad. Speech recognition, translation and summaries happen on the device, and after a one-time model download it works in airplane mode.

iPhone & iPadApp Store

Version 2.0.1, updated 2026-10-21 (App Store Japan)

What it does

What VoiceLink AI Translator does.

01

100% on-device, offline

Your voice and conversations are never uploaded. No account, no ads. Good for confidential meetings and travel without data.

02

Line by line

Original and translation appear side by side as people talk. Swap languages with one tap for two-way conversations.

03

20 languages

Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean are detected automatically; others are chosen manually.

04

Long meeting transcripts

Record one- to two-hour meetings, in the background, with Live Activities.

05

Start instantly

From Control Center, the Action Button, Shortcuts or Siri.

Pricing

Microphone interpreting is free. VoiceLink Pro (audio from other apps, floating subtitles, read-aloud, summaries) is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and supports Family Sharing.

Good to know

  • Requires iOS 26 or later. Interpreting other apps needs iOS 27; summaries need Apple Intelligence.
  • Older devices may be slower or run warm.
