Flagship

At the centre:
Gokaku! Quest.

A study app born from our own family's entrance-exam years. Science, social studies, Japanese and math, plus real-estate, financial planning, bookkeeping, IT Passport, SPI3, Japanese, English and driving theory, in one app.

  • Illustrated atlases for all 12 courses (6,074 pages)
  • Question art, illustrated explanations and read-aloud audio
  • A kingdom map that grows as you learn
  • Switch profiles so siblings keep separate records

Level 40 and 50 questions are free; levels 60 and 70 and the full atlases are Pro. In-app purchases and ads.

FAQ

Before you start.

Who is TrailFusion AI?

An indie studio run by Hiroaki Yuasa. On the App Store, the publisher name is "hiroaki yuasa".

Are the apps free?

All apps are free to download. Some features and courses are in-app purchases; check the purchase screen in each app for details and prices.

How do I contact you?

Use the support page or email trailfusionai@gmail.com. App-specific terms and privacy policies are listed on the support page.

Are the apps in English?

Several apps support English, including Reel Make, VoiceLink, Word Blaster and BuddyTalk. Gokaku! Quest is built around Japanese courses.
