FAQ

Find your first app.

Which app should I try first?

Choose Reel Make to create a narrated video, VoiceLink AI Translator to translate a conversation, Japanese Quest to learn Japanese, English Quest for vocabulary and grammar, or BuddyTalk to practise speaking English.

Are Japanese Quest and English Quest separate downloads?

Both are language-learning courses inside Gokaku Quest. Download Gokaku Quest, then choose Japanese or English in the app. The store name may vary by region.

Can I use these apps in English?

These apps offer English or multilingual features. The learning language and the language of explanations are different: Japanese Quest teaches Japanese, while English Quest and BuddyTalk teach English. Check each app for supported languages and devices.

Are the apps free?

The apps are free to download. Some features and learning content require in-app purchases. VoiceLink Pro is a one-time purchase; Reel Make and BuddyTalk offer subscriptions. Check the purchase screen for current prices and terms.

Who makes the apps?

TrailFusion AI is an independent app studio run by Hiroaki Yuasa. The App Store publisher name is hiroaki yuasa. For help, email trailfusionai@gmail.com.
