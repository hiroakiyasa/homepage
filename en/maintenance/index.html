Important notes

Keep learning separate from hands-on vehicle work.

The 3D model is for understanding. Component shapes and positions are simplified for learning purposes. Actual vehicles vary by model year, model, and specification. Confirm tightening torques and service procedures in the applicable service manual and with a qualified repair facility. Maintenance records are stored in this browser, so export them as a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data.
