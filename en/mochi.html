Mochi

Talk, and your feelings
turn into words.

Mochi, a soft little companion, is waiting in your room. Tell it about your day, the good parts and the cloudy ones. Mochi listens, nods and reacts with its face and body.

In App Store review, coming soon

What it does

What Mochi does.

01

Real-time talk, on the device

Japanese speech recognition, replies and speech run on the device. No account or external AI service is needed.

02

Faces and moves that match the mood

19 expressions and 28 movements respond to the feeling in what you say.

03

A drawer of words

Cards that borrow ideas from old thinkers to put cloudy feelings into words.

04

Memories and a mood calendar

A diary, mood records, seasonal letters and a year in review.

05

Care, dress-up and decorating

Pet, feed, play and breathe together; collect leaves for hats and furniture in a 3D room.

06

For children and adults

Child mode uses simpler words and hides the shop.

Pricing

Care, conversation, the diary and the leaf shop are free. The only purchases are two one-time room themes, Sakura Room and Starlight Room.

Good to know

  • Currently in App Store review.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your diary stays on your device.
  • Conversation is in Japanese and needs a supported device.
  • Not a medical service; support contacts are shown when feelings are very hard.
