Hacking Timer

★5.01 ratings

Every 30 minutes or every hour,
a gentle time check.

Marks time at 30-minute or 1-hour intervals within the hours and days you choose, as repeating iPhone alarms or notifications on Apple Watch.

iPhone & iPadApp Store

Version 1.4.0, updated 2026-10-05 (App Store Japan)

What it does

What Hacking Timer does.

01

Repeating iPhone alarms

Weekly schedules by day and time (iOS 26 or later), including overnight and 24-hour windows.

02

Apple Watch notifications

Set times, days and vibration patterns directly on Watch.

03

On time or a little early

Alert at the time or a chosen number of minutes before.

04

No account

Settings stay on your devices and sync between iPhone and Watch. No data collection or ad tracking.

Made for

  • Structuring study or work time
  • A discreet buzz on the wrist
  • A weekly routine

Pricing

No account or subscription required.

Good to know

  • Requires iOS 17; iPhone alarms need iOS 26; Watch notifications need watchOS 11.4.
