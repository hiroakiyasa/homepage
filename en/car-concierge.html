The more you drive,

Where to rest tonight, how much this car park costs, where the nearest EV charger is and where to go next: one map for road trips in Japan, from car camping to EV travel and weekend drives.

What it does

What Car Concierge does.

01

Find a place to rest tonight

Roadside stations, RV parks, service areas, car parks, hot springs, toilets and convenience stores on one map.

02

Search car parks and compare fees

Over 100,000 car parks across Japan. Compare fees for the time you plan to stay and filter by type or free parking.

03

EV charging with time estimates

Find fast and normal chargers and estimate charging time, cost and range from your car, battery level and charger output.

04

Keep a travel log

With your consent, the routes you drove and places you visited are recorded on the device and grow into your own map.

05

Collect roadside stations and scenic roads

Roadside stations, prefectures, famous spots, mountain passes and scenic roads as challenges to complete.

06

Rankings for your next trip

Ten measures such as distance, roadside stations, hot springs and streaks help you choose the next drive.

Made for

  • Car campers looking for a place to stay tonight
  • Drivers comparing coin-parking fees
  • EV drivers who need chargers and charging times
  • Anyone who loves roadside stations and scenic roads

Pricing

The map, basic parking search, fee comparison, facilities, challenges and the basic travel log are free with no ads. PRO adds advanced search, offline maps and more.

Good to know

  • Information and calculations are for reference. Check official sources for actual fees, hours, facilities and road conditions.
  • Do not use the app while driving.
  • Available on the Japanese App Store only.
