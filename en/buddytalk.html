BuddyTalk

Start with a short reply.
Keep the conversation going.

Practise English with AI characters. Say or type a reply, hear your partners answer, and get help with what to say next. Start with a simple hello and build at your own pace.

iPhone & iPadApp Store

See the App Store for current version, devices and availability.

What it does

What BuddyTalk does.

01

500 beginner conversation scenes

Start with greetings, introductions, thanks and everyday small talk. Suggested replies and word meanings help you begin.

02

One or two AI conversation partners

Choose from 1,500 scenes: 750 with one AI partner and 750 with two. These are AI role-plays, not live chats with other learners.

03

Lessons, stories and grammar

Practise useful expressions, revisit their meaning and return to your saved progress.

04

Guidance in a familiar language

Choose from 10 support languages and 11 language settings. Practice dialogue stays in English.

05

Clear choices for AI conversation

Cloud AI is the default, with an explanation and consent before the first request. Supported high-end devices can optionally download a model to practise offline.

How it works

  1. 1
    Choose your support language

    Read guidance in a familiar language. The conversation itself stays in English.

  2. 2
    Pick a scene

    Begin with an everyday greeting or a situation you want to practise.

  3. 3
    Try a short reply

    Speak or type, listen to the AI partner and use the learning support to continue.

Made for

  • Beginners who want to start speaking English
  • Learners who want to practise before a real conversation
  • People fitting English practice into short breaks

Pricing

Start with free beginner content and learning support on the first three replies of each conversation. BuddyTalk Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription that unlocks all lessons, stories and levels, plus learning support on every reply. Current regional prices are shown before purchase.

Good to know

  • Requires iOS or iPadOS 17.6 or later and an internet connection for cloud AI conversation. Optional offline models require a supported high-end device and a large initial download.
  • AI replies can contain mistakes. Practice and feedback do not guarantee an exam result.
  • Earlier one-time purchases keep working and can be restored.
