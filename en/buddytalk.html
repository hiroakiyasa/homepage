Questions about 2.0.0

Is version 2.0.0 available on the App Store yet?

As of October 31, 2026, version 2.0.0 is being prepared for App Review. The current public version is 1.4.6. The new features and purchase options described here are planned for 2.0.0; a release date has not been confirmed. Check the App Store for availability.

What can I do offline?

After downloading the required AI model and voice files, supported devices process AI conversations, translations, corrections and reply assistance locally. Voice input also needs compatible on-device speech resources. Initial downloads, purchases and restoration, advertising and privacy-choice services, and optional web searches or external links need a connection. Offline ads are skipped so learning can continue.

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Community Talk levels 1–5 offer 500 free beginner scenes starting with short greetings such as Hi, Good morning and Thanks. Begin with a few words, then progress to brief exchanges, everyday small talk and familiar requests. The full collection is arranged across levels 1–10.

Who joins two- and three-person conversations?

Two people means the learner and one AI character; three means the learner and two AI characters. There are 750 two-person and 750 three-person scenarios, for 1,500 in total. Community Talk is not a live chat service with other users. There are no four-person scenarios.

Which languages can I use to learn English?

The learning language is English. Guidance is available in 10 languages across 11 settings: English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, French for France and Canada, German, Spanish, Korean, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese and Russian. Practice stays in English while interface text, tasks and short reply meanings provide support. Some supplemental translations are generated on the device when requested.

What is free, and what does Premium include?

The 500 beginner Community Talk scenes at levels 1–5, basic interface, catalog titles and task guidance in your support language are free. Premium is a one-time purchase for conversation stages 6–10, grammar stages 4–6, Community levels 6–10, Premium stories and the lower conversation panel’s translations, corrections, vocabulary and reply suggestions. It also removes ads. A separate one-time Remove Ads purchase is available.

What device and storage do I need?

Version 2.0.0 requires iOS/iPadOS 17.6 or later and compatible hardware. At least 8 GB RAM is recommended. Initial AI and voice downloads total about 1.6–5.7 GB, depending on the model selected for your device. Wi-Fi and at least 10 GB free storage during setup are recommended; 10 GB is not a fixed mandatory minimum. Speed varies with the device, battery and operating conditions.

What happens to existing purchases and progress?

Saved learning progress is preserved. Existing Remove Ads purchases can be restored, and eligible buyers can choose a Premium upgrade. An earlier monthly or yearly subscription keeps its verified access while active. An update or a new one-time purchase does not automatically cancel that subscription or turn it into a lifetime purchase. Check Apple’s subscription management for its status.
