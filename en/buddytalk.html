What it does

What BuddyTalk does.

01

AI conversation practice

10+ AI tutors with American, British and Australian accents, pronunciation scores and grammar fixes in real time.

02

90+ stories

Six categories from daily life to business, three levels. Learn expressions in context.

03

Seven daily challenges

Listening, translation, pronunciation, vocabulary, dictation, story quizzes and review, with a bean character that grows.

04

Weekly leagues

Ten tiers from Bronze to Diamond in groups of 30.

05

Word Blaster

A shooting game for vocabulary.
