Reel Make

★5.07 ratings

Pick your photos.
Get a reel with narration.

Reel Make turns a few photos or short clips on your phone into a finished vertical reel. AI handles structure, narration, captions and cuts, so no editing skills are needed.

iPhone & iPadApp StoreAndroidGoogle Play

Version 0.2.4, updated 2026-10-16 (App Store Japan)

What it does

What Reel Make does.

01

AI builds the structure and script

It orders your media and writes a narration script that fits the content.

02

Natural voice narration

Up to 60 multilingual voices with speaker, tone and speed. Preview before export.

03

Captions synced to the voice

Karaoke-style captions with designs, text animations, colours and sizes you can adjust.

04

Cuts and background music

Cut editing and music mixing are automatic, and you can balance narration and music.

05

Export for any platform

Vertical 9:16 and other ratios, plus captions for each social network copied in one tap.

Pricing

Free exports up to 30 seconds. Pro (45 seconds, 1080p, no watermark) and MAX (90 seconds) are monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscriptions; see the purchase screen for prices.

Good to know

  • Direct posting and scheduling only happen if you choose to connect a social account.
