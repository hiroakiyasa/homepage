Before you start

Are Japanese Quest and English Quest separate apps?

They are two courses inside Gokaku Quest. Download the same app, then choose Japanese or English. The app name shown by the store may vary by region.

Can I study with English explanations?

Yes. English explanations are supported in Japanese Quest and English Quest. The language you are learning is separate from the language used for explanations. Some interface labels and illustrated materials remain in Japanese. Available lessons and explanation languages can vary by content, OS and app version.

Are the Japanese entrance-exam and qualification courses in English?

The international learning pages cover Japanese Quest and English Quest only. The Japanese entrance-exam and qualification courses use Japanese; they are not presented here as English-language courses.

Is everything free?

The app is free to download. You can try the available free questions. Additional questions, illustrated guides, audio and other features may require an in-app purchase. Check the app for the current scope, plans and prices.

Does the app guarantee a JLPT or JFT-Basic result?

No. These are independent study aids with original learning material. They are not official exam apps and do not guarantee a test result. Use the course alongside the official exam information and other study materials.
